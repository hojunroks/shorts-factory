Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-01

Shorts Factory is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel 장면한입 (@scenehanip-r7s). It runs on the owner's own computer.

What it accesses

  • YouTube upload (youtube.upload): to upload videos the owner has approved.
  • YouTube read-only (youtube.readonly): to confirm which channel is connected and to read that channel's public video statistics.

What it stores

The OAuth refresh token is stored in the macOS Keychain on the owner's computer. Channel IDs, video IDs and statistics are stored in a local database on the same computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's YouTube APIs, and no data is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at Google account permissions, or by disconnecting the channel in the tool, which deletes the stored token.
